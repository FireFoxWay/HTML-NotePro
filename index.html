§ 01 · The Idea

Everything, one page

No tabs. No app-switching. One scroll.

NotePro gathers a calendar, a to-do list, study notes, a file vault, and a chat room into a single vertical workspace. Scroll from your morning schedule to tonight's homework without ever leaving the page.

§ 02 · Home

A clock, not a feed

The workspace opens on time itself.

Your day begins with a large, quiet clock and today's date — no notifications, no noise, nothing begging for attention. A soft cue simply invites you to scroll.

§ 03 · Calendar

Plans with places attached

Events that know where and when.

Add an event with a time, a web link, and a location. Locations turn into one-tap Google Maps directions; links keep meeting URLs a click away. Days with plans get a quiet dot on the month grid.

§ 04 · To-Do

Capture, check, clear

Small tasks, satisfying strikethroughs.

Type a task, press Enter, and it's captured. Check it off to strike it through, then sweep everything finished away with one Clear Completed. A running count keeps score of open versus done.

§ 05 · Study Notes

Key points that stick

One bullet per line, sorted by subject.

Pick a subject, name the topic, and write your key points — each line becomes a clean bullet on a dated card. Perfect for revision the night before a test.

§ 06 · Chat

Real names, tiny rooms

A room code shared between friends.

Agree on a room code, open the site, and talk. Every message travels under your account name — no impostors, no renaming mid-conversation. A red dot on the navigation rail tells you when something arrived while you were elsewhere.

§ 07 · File Vault

Files that never leave

Stored in your browser, not on a server.

Save PDFs, music, and video into the vault and they stay on your device. Preview PDF, MP3, and MP4 right on the page; download anything back out whenever you need it.

§ 08 · Terminal

Drive it with words

For keyboard people.

Type goto chat to jump across the page, wallpaper neon to retheme the scene, or stats to count your notes, tasks, and files — a tiny command line built into the workspace.

§ 09 · Scenes

A background that breathes

A dotted forest, or a spiral galaxy.

Behind everything runs a living 3D scene — a firefly-lit forest or this galaxy — that glides as you scroll, with matching ambient sound you can switch on from the corner. Six color themes tint it to taste.

§ 10 · Your Account

One identity, everywhere

Sign up once — with email, Google, or Apple.

Your display name follows you into chat, so friends always know it's really you. Rename yourself or sign out any time from the account menu in the corner.
